Step by step
Import your case list
Paste the list you work from each day. New cases are added; cases you already have keep all their notes and actions.
1
Copy your list

Any of these works — the app looks for the 7–10 digit case number on each line and treats the words next to it as the client name:

  • From Excel or Google Sheets: select the rows (case number + name columns) and press Ctrl+C.
  • From an email, Teams or your case system: copy the lines as they are.
  • From a screenshot: give the screenshot to an AI assistant (Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude…) together with the prompt below, then copy its answer.
AI prompt for turning a screenshot into a list
Paste it into the AI together with your screenshot.
2
Paste it here

One case per line. Separate the columns with |, a tab, or a comma. Dates are optional (they become the 1st call's due date).

10482914 | Sarah Jenkins | 2026-10-02 | Mortgage renewal 10492837 Alice Wang 10594832, Robert Fox
You can also drop a file here.
3
Check what will change
Starting over? Clear your current list before importing. Deletes every case you have now, with their notes. You can undo straight after.
